triqui - tuning at runtime

Four bars at two, three, four and five tiles above the floor, and two gaps two and three tiles wide. With the default tuning the character reaches only the first bar and clears both gaps. Slider changes apply on the next physics step, because the tuning object is read at the start of every step.
